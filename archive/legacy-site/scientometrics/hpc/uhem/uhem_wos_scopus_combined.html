Why this Union page differs
Union pages begin with provenance because the merge rule defines every downstream total. Recent-paper and researcher leaderboards are omitted: author identifiers are not complete across both source arms.
Why combine them. Neither available source arm alone is complete, and within the supplied datasets the gaps are mostly metadata: papers are usually present in the other available source but arrive without a usable acknowledgment. The WoS arm is a supplied ULAKBİM record subset, not a complete mirror of the current public Core Collection, so "WoS-absent" here means absent from that local subset. Scopus keeps a single funder per paper, so a centre can be dropped from a record it otherwise holds; WoS carries acknowledgment text for only about a third of its records, but keeps it in full when it does. Those two failures are independent, so the union recovers work neither would report alone. It remains a lower bound — a paper whose acknowledgment is missing from both is invisible to both. For records shared across databases, the Scopus document type is retained; a record Scopus classifies as a review or conference paper does not re-enter the journal-article total merely because WoS labels it Article. That is why the retained WoS component can be slightly smaller than the WoS-only dashboard.

Retrieval gaps affect high-impact work. Among the ten most-cited articles here, 5 entered this corpus only through the Web of Science source arm and 3 only through the Scopus source arm. Each arm is acknowledgment-defined and can include reviewed paper-level evidence. “Only” does not necessarily mean the article is absent from the other database, whose funding metadata may differ. The single most-cited article in this corpus — Two- and One-Dimensional Honeycomb Structures of Silicon and Germanium (2009, 3,060 citations) — entered this acknowledgment corpus only through the Web of Science source arm; a Scopus acknowledgment-only study would omit it.

Journal quality

Share of ranked articles by SCImago SJR quartile, matched to each paper's publication year. The number above each bar is that year's ranked article count.
SCImago for every paper, including the WoS ones. WoS ships its own JCR-style categories, but mixing two quartile systems in one column would make the distribution meaningless — a "Q1" would mean different things in different rows. Scopus records join SJR on source-id first, WoS-only records on ISSN; the two systems agree on 99% of the papers both databases hold. 10 of 843 articles are in journals SJR does not rank.

Citations per year

For papers both databases hold, the higher of the two counts — each index sees only its own citing papers, so neither is definitive and the larger is the better lower bound. Counts are never summed.

Turkish institutions

By number of articles acknowledging UHeM, across both databases. 187 institutions in total.
Scopus names Turkish institutions in Turkish ("Cumhuriyet Üniversitesi"), WoS in English ("Cumhuriyet University"), so the two are merged by a normalisation that folds Turkish characters, translates institutional words and compares tokens without regard to order. It matches the large institutions reliably; the long tail of single-paper affiliations may still hold near-duplicates.

Method. Union of the Scopus and Web of Science acknowledgment corpora, journal articles only. Merged on DOI (lowercased); 1 records carry no DOI and were merged on a normalised title instead. Publication year taken from Scopus where present, else WoS — the two disagree on 9 papers. Citations are the higher of the two counts, never the sum. A paper counts as open access if either database says so. Quartiles are SCImago SJR throughout, matched to each paper's publication year.

Researcher summaries are deliberately absent: only one source arm carries dependable author identifiers here, so a union-wide ranking would describe a subset while appearing complete. Institution counts are retained because both arms provide affiliation strings; their cross-source normalisation and remaining long-tail limitations are stated beside that ranking.
