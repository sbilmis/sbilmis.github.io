Journal quality

Journal quality

Share of ranked articles by WoS/JCR quartile (Clarivate's own Journal Citation Reports Q1-Q4, not SCImago SJR — this is the one page on this site that uses it), matched to each paper's publication year. Click a segment for that year+quartile's articles. The number above each bar is that year's ranked article count — a 100% bar built on two papers says very little.
Quartiles are joined by ISSN only against data/external/wos_jcr/bs_ytb_wos_journal.json, whose newest release is 2025 (JCR for a year is published the following year, so the newest table always lags the newest papers). A paper published after 2025 is scored against the 2025 table — the newest one available, not a guess at what its actual 2026+ category/quartile will be once JCR catches up. That affects 59 ranked articles here (everything published in 2026 or later). Separately, 1 of 736 articles are in a journal JCR does not rank in any year (an ISSN absent from JCR entirely, not a lookup failure). A journal's JCR quartile is per subject category, and a journal usually sits in more than one; the best quartile is used here, as SCImago's is elsewhere on this site. Of the 312 ranked articles whose category breakdown is known (JCR publishes it from 2023), 78 (25%) are in a journal whose quartile differs between its categories — those would read lower under a different category choice. Every other page on this site — Scopus, the comparisons, the combined corpus — still uses SCImago SJR throughout, so a paper's quartile can legitimately read differently here than on those pages; the two systems are never mixed in one column.

Where it began
2008
First Web of Science-retrieved acknowledgment in this extracted corpus
Ozen, Cihan et al., “A DFT study on the mechanism of cyclopropanation via Cu(acac)2-catalyzed diazo ester decomposition”, ORGANOMETALLICS (2008-09).
Source-specific milestone: a different database can begin later because the record itself or its acknowledgment metadata is absent; this is not evidence that the centre's use began later. Web of Science began indexing funding-acknowledgment text in August 2008. Earlier papers carry none at all, so no acknowledgment search can reach them whatever the wording — in this snapshot 0.8% of 2007 records hold any funding text, against 9.9% in 2008 and 28–38% from 2009 onward. Pre-2008 work appears here only where the record was loaded years after publication. Sparse early years are a property of the index, not evidence that little was acknowledged.
Methodology and limits
Method. Within this project's archived Web of Science Core Collection snapshot, funding-acknowledgment text is matched against the centre's name variants — the same terms the Scopus arm uses. Counts are a lower bound: only written acknowledgments present in the project snapshot can be found.

Which names count. The centre is cited as UHeM, as its former acronym UYBHM, and by its English long form. The long form cannot be used unqualified — “National Center for High Performance Computing” also names Taiwan's national centre and returns thousands of unrelated records — so it counts only in its country-qualified form. A centre's naming history is part of the query, not a preprocessing detail.

Instrument limits. Citations are WoS Core Collection as of 2026-07-21 and are not live. Open access here is WoS's gold-only flag, so it is not comparable with a Scopus or OpenAIRE OA share. Quartiles are Clarivate's own JCR Q1-Q4 (not SCImago SJR), matched by ISSN to the paper's publication year, 1999-2025.
