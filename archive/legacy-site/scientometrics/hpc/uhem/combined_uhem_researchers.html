How to read the two paper columns. Papers is whole counting: every co-author receives one full paper. Fractional paper share divides each paper equally among all its authors; for example, one four-author paper contributes 0.25. The fractional value is therefore a collaboration-adjusted share, not another paper count.
Definitions and limits. Citations are whole-counted sums over each researcher's UHeM papers. Q1 share is calculated among that researcher's SJR-ranked papers. First and last are active years in this corpus. Researchers are aggregated by surname and first initial, the common identity available across Scopus and Web of Science. Only authors linked to a Turkish affiliation on the paper are counted; on WoS-only mixed-country papers, per-author affiliations are unavailable, so uncertain authors are not guessed.
