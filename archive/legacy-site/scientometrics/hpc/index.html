National high-performance computing

These dashboards are built from funding-acknowledgement text: a paper counts when its authors say the centre helped produce it. That is a different question from who employs the authors, and it is the only way to measure what a shared facility enables. Each dashboard reports the combined Scopus + Web of Science total: neither database alone finds every acknowledgement, so both are used and the overlap is counted once. The WLCG page below is counted differently — by collaboration membership — and its total is not addable to the other two.
