Journal quality

Journal quality

Share of ranked articles by WoS/JCR quartile (Clarivate's own Journal Citation Reports Q1-Q4, not SCImago SJR — this is the one page on this site that uses it), matched to each paper's publication year. Click a segment for that year+quartile's articles. The number above each bar is that year's ranked article count — a 100% bar built on two papers says very little.
Quartiles are joined by ISSN only against data/external/wos_jcr/bs_ytb_wos_journal.json, whose newest release is 2025 (JCR for a year is published the following year, so the newest table always lags the newest papers). A paper published after 2025 is scored against the 2025 table — the newest one available, not a guess at what its actual 2026+ category/quartile will be once JCR catches up. That affects 163 ranked articles here (everything published in 2026 or later). Separately, 1 of 1,951 articles are in a journal JCR does not rank in any year (an ISSN absent from JCR entirely, not a lookup failure). A journal's JCR quartile is per subject category, and a journal usually sits in more than one; the best quartile is used here, as SCImago's is elsewhere on this site. Of the 866 ranked articles whose category breakdown is known (JCR publishes it from 2023), 179 (21%) are in a journal whose quartile differs between its categories — those would read lower under a different category choice. Every other page on this site — Scopus, the comparisons, the combined corpus — still uses SCImago SJR throughout, so a paper's quartile can legitimately read differently here than on those pages; the two systems are never mixed in one column.

First Web of Science-indexed acknowledgment in this extracted corpus
Gokturkler, Gokhan et al., “Geophysical investigation of a landslide:: The Altindag landslide site, Izmir (western Turkey)”, JOURNAL OF APPLIED GEOPHYSICS (2008-06).
Methodology and limits
Method. Records whose WoS funding-acknowledgment text matches the centre's name variants — the same terms the Scopus arm uses. Counts are a lower bound: only written acknowledgments can be found. Citations are WoS Core Collection as of 2026-07-21 and are not live. Open access here is WoS's gold-only flag, so it is not comparable with a Scopus or OpenAIRE OA share. Quartiles are Clarivate's own JCR Q1-Q4 (not SCImago SJR), matched by ISSN to the paper's publication year, 1999-2025.
