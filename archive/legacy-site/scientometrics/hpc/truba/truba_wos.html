Journal quality

Journal quality

Share of ranked articles by WoS/JCR quartile (Clarivate's own Journal Citation Reports Q1-Q4, not SCImago SJR — this is the one page on this site that uses it), matched to each paper's publication year. Click a segment for that year+quartile's articles. The number above each bar is that year's ranked article count — a 100% bar built on two papers says very little.
Quartiles are joined by ISSN only against data/external/wos_jcr/bs_ytb_wos_journal.json, whose newest release is 2025 (JCR for a year is published the following year, so the newest table always lags the newest papers). A paper published after 2025 is scored against the 2025 table — the newest one available, not a guess at what its actual 2026+ category/quartile will be once JCR catches up. That affects 165 ranked articles here (everything published in 2026 or later). Separately, 1 of 2,044 articles are in a journal JCR does not rank in any year (an ISSN absent from JCR entirely, not a lookup failure). A journal's JCR quartile is per subject category, and a journal usually sits in more than one; the best quartile is used here, as SCImago's is elsewhere on this site. Of the 881 ranked articles whose category breakdown is known (JCR publishes it from 2023), 182 (21%) are in a journal whose quartile differs between its categories — those would read lower under a different category choice. Every other page on this site — Scopus, the comparisons, the combined corpus — still uses SCImago SJR throughout, so a paper's quartile can legitimately read differently here than on those pages; the two systems are never mixed in one column.

Where it began
2005
First Web of Science-retrieved acknowledgment in this extracted corpus
Senger, R. Tugrul et al., “Magic Gold Nanotubes”, TURKISH JOURNAL OF PHYSICS (2005-01).
Source-specific milestone: a different database can begin later because the record itself or its acknowledgment metadata is absent; this is not evidence that the centre's use began later. Web of Science began indexing funding-acknowledgment text in August 2008. Earlier papers carry none at all, so no acknowledgment search can reach them whatever the wording — in this snapshot 0.8% of 2007 records hold any funding text, against 9.9% in 2008 and 28–38% from 2009 onward. Pre-2008 work appears here only where the record was loaded years after publication. Sparse early years are a property of the index, not evidence that little was acknowledged.
Methodology and limits
Method. Within this project's archived Web of Science Core Collection snapshot, funding-acknowledgment text is matched against the centre's name variants — the same terms the Scopus arm uses. Counts are a lower bound: only written acknowledgments present in the project snapshot can be found.

Which names count. The centre has been renamed twice — High Performance Computing Center (pre-2012), … and Grid Computing Center, and … and Network Computing Center (current) — and authors still use all three. Only the middle form contains “and Grid”, so a query resting on that phrase alone misses the other two. The bare English name cannot be a search term (it matches hundreds of unrelated centres worldwide, İTÜ's UHeM among them), so it counts only when anchored to “ULAKBİM” in the same breath.

Ambiguous credits. A bare “ULAKBİM” credit is ambiguous: the same body runs the national HPC centre and the national open-access and licensing agreements. Those records are judged on the sentence that names ULAKBİM — computing vocabulary confirms it, publishing vocabulary rejects it, anything else stays unclear and is excluded. The same rule now runs on both database arms; applying it to only one of them silently biased the comparison between them. On this snapshot 203 records reached that test: 77 confirmed as computing acknowledgments and kept, 66 rejected as open-access or licensing credits, 60 left unclear and excluded.

Instrument limits. Citations are WoS Core Collection as of 2026-07-21 and are not live. Open access here is WoS's gold-only flag, so it is not comparable with a Scopus or OpenAIRE OA share.
